Who We Are

Arivuu is a future ready career counselling ecosystem designed for schools, students, and parents. We deliver NEP aligned, AI powered career guidance that blends scientific precision with human expertise. With accurate psychometric assessments, personalised reports, scholarship insights, and one on one counselling, Arivuu helps learners discover strengths, explore careers, and build confident futures.

Why Arivuu

Arivuu stands out through its scientific foundation and holistic approach. Our platform is built on validated psychometric tools and modern analytics, ensuring guidance that is structured, unbiased, and reliable. Unlike traditional counselling, Arivuu empowers schools with dashboards, insights, and scalable
programs, while students and parents gain clarity and confidence in every decision.

Arivuu for Everyone

India's education landscape is diverse, and opportunities must be accessible to all. Arivuu dissolves barriers of geography, language, and resources by making career guidance available to every child. Whether in high school or preparing for college, our integrated ecosystem ensures that students are guided, parents are supported, and schools are empowered to deliver career clarity at scale.
